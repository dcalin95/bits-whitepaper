# Roadmap

### Overview

The BitSwapDEX AI roadmap outlines a strategic and phased path to deliver an AI-powered decentralized exchange built on the Stacks blockchain and anchored to Bitcoin via Proof-of-Transfer (PoX). The roadmap balances technical innovation, security, liquidity sustainability, and community participation.

> **Disclaimer:** Timelines and strategies may evolve based on audits, market conditions, community feedback, and regulatory factors. The roadmap is designed for long-term resilience and transparency.

***

### 🧱 Phase 1 – Foundation & Strategic Setup _(Q2–Q3 2025)_

* Core team assembled (blockchain engineers, AI developers, DeFi strategists).
* Initial contract suite drafted: `Node.sol`, `CellManager.sol`, `ReferralReward.sol`.
* Whitepaper v1.0 released: tokenomics, vesting mechanics, AI governance model.
* Official brand and website launched.
* Communications established: Telegram, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Medium.
* AI prototypes initiated: predictive liquidity optimization, routing simulations.

***

### 🪙 Phase 2 – Presale Campaign _(Q1–Q3 2026)_

* **ERC20 $BITS already deployed on BSC.**
* Modular presale spans **8–9 months**, with dynamic round lengths:
  * Initial rounds: \~1 month duration.
  * Later rounds: \~2 weeks if performance targets are met.
* Early supporters receive discounted allocations.
* Referral logic live; KYC/AML compliant.
* Strategic pauses for outreach, education, and infrastructure.
* Closure: investor snapshot, public presale report, vesting contracts deployed.

***

### 🧪 Phase 3 – TestNet & Security Validation _(Q4 2026)_

* Smart contracts deployed to **Stacks TestNet** and **BSC TestNet**.
* Modules tested: swaps, LP staking, rewards, governance voting prototypes.
* AI components integrated: routing prediction and liquidity rebalancing.
* Synthetic load stress testing.
* Third-party audits (CertiK, Trail of Bits, Hacken); reports made public.

***

### 🌐 Phase 4 – Initial DEX Launch on BSC _(Q1 2027)_

* **Launch of $BITS on PancakeSwap**, seeded with presale liquidity.
* Vesting logic enforced for investor allocations.
* Market protection: anti-whale mechanics and MEV/front-running mitigation.
* AI-based swap monitoring and liquidity depth analytics activated.
* Governance v0 via Snapshot (symbolic, off-chain proposals).

***

### 🔁 Phase 5 – Stacks Mainnet Migration _(Q2 2027)_

* $BITS re-deployed as a native **Clarity smart contract** on Stacks.
* **1:1 bridge** enabled: ERC20 ↔ Stacks-native $BITS.
* On-chain proof of supply parity.
* Liquidity pools launched on Stacks:
  * **BITS/BTC** (primary Bitcoin pair)
  * **BITS/STX** (Stacks ecosystem pair)
  * **BITS/USDT (bridged)** for stablecoin hedging.
* Migration dashboard goes live: one-click conversion, balance detection, transparent stats.

***

### 🧠 Phase 6 – Bitcoin-Native DEX Expansion _(Q3 2027)_

* BitSwapDEX AI fully operational on **Stacks Mainnet**, secured via PoX.
* Direct **BITS/BTC** trading with native pools and real-time AI rebalancing.
* Full **on-chain governance (v1)** using Clarity + predictive simulation models.
* **Cross-chain routing pilots**: Ethereum, BSC, Solana ↔ Stacks.
* Launch of community **grants program** for AI bots, dashboards, LP analytics tools.

***

### 🏦 Phase 7 – Institutional Onboarding & CEX Listings _(Q4 2027 – Q1 2028)_

* Institutional suite released:
  * AI-enhanced limit orders
  * DeFi risk management tools
  * Compliance-ready reporting modules
* Margin and leverage trading introduced (jurisdiction dependent).
* Global expansion via hackathons, community campaigns, and educational summits.
* **Centralized Exchange (CEX) listing strategy**:
  * **Q4 2027:** Gate.io, KuCoin, OKX applications.
  * **Q1 2028:** Tier-1 applications: Binance, Coinbase, Crypto.com, Kraken.
* Market makers onboarded to manage liquidity across DEX and CEX environments.

***

### 🌱 Phase 8 – Continuous Evolution _(Q2 2028+)_

* Deep-learning upgrades to AI routing, LP optimization, and risk modeling.
* Exploration of **Stacks L2 or rollup solutions** for long-term scalability.
* Launch of sustainability pools with AI-adjusted incentives.
* Ongoing Tier-1 CEX listing strategy as liquidity and usage scale.

***

### 🔑 Technical Highlight – ERC20 to Stacks-Native Migration

* ERC20 $BITS ↔ Stacks-native $BITS bridge with 1:1 conversion ratio.
* Full utility migration: governance, liquidity, staking, and rewards now run **natively on Stacks**.
* Clarity contracts enforce supply parity and anchor all activity to Bitcoin via PoX.
*   Forms the foundation of a **Bitcoin-native, AI-automated decentralized exchange**.\
