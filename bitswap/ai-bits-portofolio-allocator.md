### 3. AI Portfolio Allocator (Wealth Engine Core)

The core of the system controls the user’s portfolio through multiple intelligent modules.

#### 3.1 Rebalancing Engine

Monitors the difference between **actual allocation** and **target allocation**, and performs incremental rebalancing only when required.

#### 3.2 Market Regime Classifier

Detects market environments such as:

* bullish trend
* bearish trend
* sideways/consolidation
* high-volatility phase
* post-crash recovery

Allocations are adjusted according to regime conditions.

#### 3.3 Micro-Adjustment System

Performs small, periodic “micro-trades” every few hours where allowed.\
Focus:

* minimize risk,
* reduce slippage,
* keep fees low,
* gradually improve long-term performance.

#### 3.4 AI Learning Loop

The model evaluates the results of its actions and gradually improves:

* strategy selection,
* threshold sensitivity,
* allocations,
* timing.

The learning loop remains fully transparent and auditable.
